# 🛠️ Source Scenarios

The SaferPlaces platform is an advanced tool for integrated analysis that utilizes mathematical models and fast processing algorithms, both raster-based and Artificial Intelligence. This technological combination allows for accurate and detailed results in a very short time. Users of the platform can quickly obtain a map of the Hazard extent, which is the risk associated with

The platform provides essential information about the Water Depth in flood-affected areas. This data is crucial for designing and implementing mitigation and intervention measures. Additionally, it offers a detailed assessment of the potential Economic Damage for each building in the risk zones. This estimate is calculated by considering the real estate asset value, expressed in euros per square meter, along with the specific vulnerability of each building. This vulnerability is described through the Damage Function, which measures the economic impact based on the structural characteristics and resilience of the affected property.

Thanks to these features, SaferPlaces positions itself as an indispensable resource for public and private entities interested in minimizing economic losses and increasing the resilience of territories against disastrous events. The ability to conduct detailed and targeted analyses allows for informed decision-making, effective response strategy planning, and the proposal of long-term interventions for the protection of the environment and urban infrastructure.

To proceed with the simulations, access the "Source Scenario" tab located at the top of the left sidebar in the interface.
