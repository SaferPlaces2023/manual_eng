# 💡 SaferPlaces Login

> Once, to access the service, enter your username and password in the login window. If you have forgotten them, you can recover them by following the recovery procedure.



<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (22).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Pagina di Log in</p></figcaption></figure>

<summary>Credential Recovery</summary>

To recover your credentials and password, click on FORGOT MY PASSWORD in the LOGIN window.

By entering your email address, the system will send the lost credentials via email.
