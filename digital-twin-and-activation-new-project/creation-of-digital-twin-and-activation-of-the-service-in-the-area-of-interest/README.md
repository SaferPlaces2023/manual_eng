# 💻 Creation of Digital Twin and activation of the service in the area of interest

Clicking the “_Create a project_” icon opens the guided interface, which helps the user activate the service and generate the Digital Twin for the designated area of interest.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-10-09 at 23.33.07.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Wizard guides the user through five steps to download or select the input layers that will enable the new project:
