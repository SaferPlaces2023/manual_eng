# STEP 1  DTM - Raster GeoTiff

To set up the study area, enter the desired location in the "area" search window.

After selecting the location or city, the map will display the area and suggest a blue rectangle as the activation area.

Using the right mouse button, you can resize or expand the rectangle.

&#x20;Once the area is defined, select the "DATASET" dropdown menu to choose the DTM you want to use to start the Digital Twin and Saferplaces project.

&#x20;The layers are ordered by spatial resolution, from the highest (LIDAR) to the lowest. Finally, choose the EPSG projection system to use in the project.

{% hint style="danger" %}
The Saferplaces platform has a pixel limit determined by the spatial resolution of the selected DTM and the size of the activated area. If this limit is exceeded, a specific error message will appear.
{% endhint %}
